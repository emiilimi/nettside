Sen Tur

Live

Worth nerding out on public transport too :) Sen Tur ("late trip") is the journey planner for anyone who wants to know when they'll actually arrive. It uses historical real-time data to work out the probability of making your connection and gives you an estimate of when you'll actually arrive.

You can also check the delay map, or look up stops, lines or departures to analyse your route.

Go to sentur.no

Live
