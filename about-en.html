It feels a bit strange to sit here writing my own website. But I'm giving it a go anyway — mostly for myself, and to try to be the role model I needed a few years ago.

So hi! My name is Emilie, I'm 19 years old, I want a better world, and I'm trying to do something about it.

Everything I learned in school taught me, in many ways, that the world is complicated, full of problems, and that we should think critically and do something about it. But looking at what everyone around me actually does, I concluded that most people don't do all that much. ("Do as I say, not as I do.") The idea that this was the life ahead of me — especially when we live in one of the world's wealthiest countries and are actively making the future worse — was something I wasn't willing to accept.

This has led me to involvement in DNT, NIO, EA, NU, GU, MGD, and more acronyms than my mum can keep track of. That is: Den Norske Turistforening (Norwegian Trekking Association), Norsk Informatikkolympiade (Norwegian Informatics Olympiad), Effective Altruism, Nature and Youth, the Green Party, Young Greens, as well as AI Youth and Safer AI.

I'm aiming for a career where I can truly make a difference — though I don't know yet what that will look like. I've also taken The Pledge and will continue donating 10% of my income to effective charities.

Right now I'm focusing on artificial intelligence — the technology that, in my view, may define our future. (more to come...) Feel free to reach out if you'd like to talk about anything at all.
